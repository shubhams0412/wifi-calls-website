"Wi-Fi calling" gets used to describe a few different things, which can make it confusing. In this guide we'll explain how internet calling works, how it compares to a regular carrier call, and when calls in Wi-Fi Calling & Texting are free and when they use coins. We'll finish with some practical tips for clear calls.

## Carrier calls vs. internet calls

A **carrier call** is the kind your phone has always made. Your voice travels over your mobile operator's network, and the call is billed according to your plan.

An **internet call**, often called a Wi-Fi call or VoIP call, sends your voice as data over an internet connection instead. That connection can be:

- **Wi-Fi**, at home, at work or on public networks, or
- **Mobile data**, when you're away from Wi-Fi.

Wi-Fi Calling & Texting uses internet calling for everything. That's why your [second phone number](/features/second-phone-number) works on the phone you already have, with no extra SIM card.

### Why that matters

Because calls go over the internet:

- You can make calls **where mobile signal is weak** but Wi-Fi is good, like a basement apartment or a large office building.
- You can stay reachable on a **US or Canadian number** wherever you have a connection.
- You can call from your browser with the [web app](/app).

## When calls are free, and when they use coins

There are two kinds of calls in the app, and they work differently.

| Type of call | Who you're calling | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| App-to-app call | Someone who also uses Wi-Fi Calling & Texting | Free |
| Phone call | Any regular phone number, mobile or landline | Uses coins |

### App-to-app calls (free)

When both people use the app, calls and chats are free. Open **Dial → App Calls**, or the **Friends** list under Contacts, and you can:

- Start a [free voice call](/features/free-app-calls)
- Start a [video call](/features/video-calls)
- Open a chat

If you allow access to your contacts, the app shows which of your contacts already use Wi-Fi Calling & Texting. Your contacts are only used to find matches and aren't stored afterward.

### Calls and texts to regular numbers (coins)

When you call or text a number that isn't on the app, the app uses **coins**. You can:

- Check your balance and top up in **Settings → Purchase Coins**
- Open **See per-minute charges** to see the cost by country for calls and SMS

Charges vary by country, so it's worth checking before you call a number abroad. See our guide to [international calling](/international-calling) for more.

## What you need for a good call

Internet calls depend on your connection more than anything else. Speed matters, but a **stable** connection matters most: voice calls don't need a lot of bandwidth, but they're sensitive to connections that drop in and out.

### Tips for clear Wi-Fi calls

- **Stay close to your router.** Walls and floors weaken Wi-Fi. If a call starts to break up, move closer or into the same room.
- **Pause big downloads.** Video streaming, game downloads or large uploads on the same network can compete with your call.
- **Choose a strong network.** If your phone is clinging to a weak Wi-Fi signal, switching to good mobile data can sound better.
- **Be careful on public Wi-Fi.** Café and hotel networks can be busy or limited. If quality drops, try mobile data.
- **Use headphones.** Wired or Bluetooth headphones reduce echo and background noise for both sides.
- **Allow microphone access.** The app asks for microphone permission so the other person can hear you. If they can't, check the permission in your phone or browser settings.

## Making a call in the app

### To another app user

1. Go to **Dial → App Calls** or **Contacts → Friends**.
2. Tap the **phone** icon for a voice call or the **video** icon for a video call.

### To a regular number

1. Open the **Dial** tab and stay on **Phone Calls**.
2. Tap the country pill to choose the country code if needed.
3. Type or paste the number and tap the **call** button.

During a call you can mute, switch to speaker, or open the keypad if you need to press numbers for a menu.

## The short version

- Internet calls travel over Wi-Fi or mobile data instead of your carrier's voice network.
- Calls and chats **between app users are free**.
