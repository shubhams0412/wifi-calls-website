Calling someone in another country isn't complicated once you know how international numbers are written. In this guide we'll cover country codes, how to format a number so the call connects, how to dial abroad in Wi-Fi Calling & Texting, and how to talk to family overseas for free when they have the app too.

## How international phone numbers are written

Every international number has three parts:

1. **The country code**, such as +1 for the United States and Canada, +44 for the United Kingdom, +91 for India or +52 for Mexico.
2. **The area or network code**, which varies by country.
3. **The local number.**

The **+** sign means "international". Writing a number in international format, like `+44 20 7946 0000`, makes it work from anywhere in the world.

### The leading zero

Many countries write local numbers with a **0** at the start, for example `020 7946 0000` in the UK. When you dial from abroad, you usually **drop that leading 0** and add the country code instead:

| Written locally | Dialed internationally |
|---|---|
| 020 7946 0000 (UK) | +44 20 7946 0000 |
| 0 98765 43210 (India) | +91 98765 43210 |

Some countries, like Italy, keep the leading zero for landlines, so if a call doesn't connect, check the local format for that country.

### Mobile vs. landline

In some countries, mobile and landline numbers have different formats or prefixes. If you're not sure, ask the person to send you their number in international format, starting with **+**.

## How to make an international call in the app

### Calling a regular phone number

1. Open the **Dial** tab and make sure **Phone Calls** is selected.
2. Tap the **country pill** (the flag and code next to the number field) and choose the country you're calling. You can search by country name or code.
3. Type the rest of the number, **without** the leading 0 if the country uses one.
4. Tap the **call** button.

You can also paste a full number that starts with `+`; the app keeps the country code you pasted.

### Calling a saved contact

Tap the **phonebook** button on the Dial tab, pick the contact, and their number fills in automatically.

## What international calls cost

Calls and texts to regular phone numbers use **coins**, and the cost **depends on the country**. Before you call:

- Go to **Settings → Purchase Coins**, open **Add coins**, and tap **See per-minute charges**.
- Switch between **Call** and **SMS** to see the coins per minute or per message for each country.
- Search for the country you want to call.

If you run out of coins mid-month, you can top up from the same screen. Charges are shown in the app because they vary by destination. For a country-by-country overview, see our [international calling](/international-calling) pages.

## Call family abroad for free

The best way to stay in touch with people overseas is often **app-to-app**. When your family or friends also use Wi-Fi Calling & Texting, calls and chats between you are free and don't use coins, whichever country you're in.

To set it up:

1. Ask them to install the app and sign in.
2. If you allow contact access, they'll appear in **Contacts → Friends** once they join.
3. Tap the **phone** icon for a [free voice call](/features/free-app-calls), the **video** icon for a [video call](/features/video-calls), or the **chat** icon to message.

You can also create a [group chat](/features/group-chat) for the whole family.

## Tips for better international calls

- **Check the time difference first.** It's easy to call at 3 a.m. by mistake.
- **Use a stable connection.** Internet calls rely on Wi-Fi or mobile data, so a strong, steady connection gives the clearest audio.
- **Save numbers in international format.** Storing contacts as `+country code number` means they work from anywhere.
- **Use the keypad for menus.** If you're calling a business abroad, open the keypad during the call to press menu options.
- **Text when a call isn't urgent.** SMS can be a simple way to reach someone in a different time zone.

## Quick reference

- Start international numbers with **+** and the **country code**.
- **Drop the leading 0** in most countries.
- Use the **country pill** on the Dial tab to pick the right country.

- Calls between app users are **free**.
