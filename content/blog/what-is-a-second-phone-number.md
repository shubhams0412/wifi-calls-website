Most of us hand out the same phone number to everyone: family, coworkers, the delivery driver, the person buying our old couch, and every website that asks for it at checkout. A **second phone number** lets you split that up. You keep your personal number for the people who matter, and use a separate number for everything else, all on the phone you already carry.

This guide explains what a second number is, how it works without a second SIM card, who tends to use one, and how to get one in Wi-Fi Calling & Texting.

## What a second phone number actually is

A second phone number is a real phone number that isn't tied to your SIM card or your carrier plan. Instead, it lives inside an app. People can call it and text it like any other number, and you can call and text from it, but it's completely separate from your personal line.

That separation is the whole point:

- **Your personal number stays private.** You decide who gets it.
- **Calls and texts stay organized.** Business, selling, dating or sign-ups don't mix with your personal conversations.
- **You stay in control.** You can block numbers on your second line without touching your personal one.

With Wi-Fi Calling & Texting, you can [choose a number](/features/choose-your-number) in the **United States or Canada**.

## How it works without a second SIM

A traditional phone number depends on a SIM card and a carrier's mobile network. A second number in an app works differently: calls and messages travel **over the internet** instead.

That means your second line works anywhere you have:

- **Wi-Fi**, at home, at work, in a café or in a hotel, or
- **Mobile data**, when you're out and about.

You don't need a second phone, a dual-SIM device or a new carrier contract. Install the app, pick a number, and it's ready to use alongside your regular number.

### Calls to other app users are free

When the person you're calling also uses Wi-Fi Calling & Texting, the call goes app-to-app and is **free**. That includes [free app-to-app calls](/features/free-app-calls), [video calls](/features/video-calls) and chat. You'll find these under **App Calls** on the Dial tab, and in the Friends list under Contacts.

### Calls and texts to regular numbers use coins

When you call or text someone who doesn't have the app, like a landline, a regular mobile number or a business, you use **coins**. You can top up in **Settings → Purchase Coins** and check the per-country costs under **See per-minute charges** before you call.

## Who uses a second phone number?

There's no single type of person who needs one. These are some of the most common reasons:

| Situation | Why a second number helps |
|---|---|
| Running a small business or side hustle | Give customers a dedicated number that isn't your personal cell |
| Selling online | Share a number in listings without exposing your real one |
| Dating | Chat with new matches before you're ready to share your personal number |
| Sign-ups and verification | Keep promotional texts and calls off your main line |
| Freelancing and contract work | Separate client calls from personal calls |
| Moving or travelling | Keep a US or Canadian number you can reach people on over Wi-Fi |

If any of those sound familiar, a second number is worth a try.

## What you can do with your second number

Once you have a number, you can:

- **Call any number** from the Dial tab, with a country picker for international numbers
- **Send texts and picture messages** to regular phone numbers
- **Chat for free** with friends who use the app, one-to-one or in [group chats](/features/group-chat)
- **Block numbers** you don't want to hear from in **Call Settings → Blocked numbers**

The Pro plan adds [voicemail](/features/voicemail) with a custom greeting, [call recording](/features/call-recording), [call forwarding](/features/call-forwarding), [anonymous calling](/features/anonymous-calling), group calls and [broadcast messages](/features/broadcast-messages). You can compare what's included on the [plans](/plans) page.

## How to get a second number in the app

Getting set up takes a few minutes:

1. **Download the app** and sign in with your phone number. You'll get a 6-digit code to verify it's you.
2. **Choose a plan.** An active subscription is needed to get a number. Pro and Lite plans start with a free trial, and the app reminds you before the trial ends.
3. **Pick your number.** Tap **Get a phone number** on the Dial tab or in Settings, choose **USA** or **Canada**, and pick a number from the list.

That's it. Your new number shows up at the top of the Dial tab, and you can start calling and texting straight away.

### Can I use it on my computer too?

Yes. Wi-Fi Calling & Texting also has a [web app](/app), so you can use the same account in your browser.

## A few things to keep in mind

- **You need an internet connection.** Calls and texts go over Wi-Fi or mobile data, so a weak connection can affect call quality.
- **Coins are only for regular numbers.** App-to-app calls and chats with other users don't use coins.
- **Your personal number isn't replaced.** Your SIM and carrier number keep working exactly as before.
