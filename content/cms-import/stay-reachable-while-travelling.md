# How to Stay Reachable While Travelling Without Roaming Charges

Travelling used to mean a hard choice: pay for a roaming plan, buy a local SIM at the airport, or go quiet until you get home. Family wants to know you landed safely, the hotel needs to confirm your booking, and your bank sends a security code at the worst possible moment. **Wi-Fi calling with a second number** keeps all of that working without a roaming plan.

This guide explains how it works, what it costs, and how to set it up before you leave.

## What is Wi-Fi calling with a second number?

Wi-Fi Calling & Texting gives you a real US or Canadian phone number that runs inside an app on the phone you already own. Calls and texts travel over Wi-Fi or mobile data instead of your carrier's network, so they work the same in a café in Lisbon as they do at home.

> **Tip:** Connect to the hotel Wi-Fi as soon as you check in. Your second number starts ringing again straight away, with no setup needed.

## Why travellers use one

There are three main reasons people set one up before a trip:

1. **No roaming bill.** Calls and texts go over Wi-Fi or data, so there's no roaming plan to buy.
2. **One number that travels with you.** Friends, family and your bank keep reaching you on the same number, wherever you are.
3. **Free calls home.** If the people you call also use the app, voice and video calls are free.

It also helps after the trip. Use your second number for hotel bookings, car rentals and tour operators, and your personal number stays off their mailing lists.

### Free calls or coins?

The kind of call decides whether it's free:

- **App-to-app calls** — when the other person also uses Wi-Fi Calling & Texting.
  - Free voice and video calls.
  - Free one-to-one and group chats.
- **Calls to regular numbers** — landlines and mobiles in the US and abroad.
  - Use coins from your balance.
  - US calls are roughly one coin per minute.

#### A quick rule of thumb

If you call the same people every day, ask them to install the app before you leave. Every call to them is then free.

For most trips, a second number over Wi-Fi is the simplest way to stay in touch.

## How to set it up before you leave

1. Download Wi-Fi Calling & Texting and create an account.
2. Choose a US or Canadian number by city.
3. Add the people you call most to your contacts.
4. Top up a few coins if you'll need to call hotels or regular numbers.
5. Share your second number with family and use it for travel bookings.

![A smartphone home screen with apps](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1512941937669-90a1b58e7e9c?w=1400&q=80 "Your second number lives in one app on the phone you already own")

Once you land, ~~buy a SIM at the airport~~ just connect to Wi-Fi and carry on.

## Tips for using it well

- Turn on **voicemail** with your own greeting so callers can leave a message while you're out exploring.
- Use *group chats* to keep everyone at home updated at once.
- Use **block numbers** to stop spam calls and texts on your second number.
- Read our guide to [calling internationally from your phone](/blog/how-to-call-internationally-from-your-phone) before you dial abroad.

When you call a regular number abroad, type it in international format: the `+` sign, the country code, then the number without its leading zero.

```text
UK mobile, written locally:   07700 900123
Dialled from the app:         +44 7700 900123
```

You can also see the coin cost for every country in the app under `Settings → Purchase Coins`, read more on the [calls to any number page](/features/phone-calls "Call any phone number over Wi-Fi") or email [support@wifiphonecalls.com](mailto:support@wifiphonecalls.com).
